The lenses

Which lens is right for you?

Every visual need has its solution. Here are the main lens types we make to measure.

Single vision

Single-focus lenses, ideal for correcting vision at one distance (far or near). The simplest and most effective solution.

Progressive

Sharp vision at all distances — far, intermediate and near — with no jumps or visible lines. The most versatile choice for presbyopia.

Bifocal

Two focus areas in the same lens, far and near, separated by a line. Convenient for those who switch between the two distances.

Photochromic

They darken automatically in sunlight and turn clear indoors. One pair of glasses for every lighting condition.

Occupational

Designed for the office and computer: they optimise intermediate and near vision, reducing eye strain.

Blue-light filter

They reduce the blue light emitted by screens and devices, for greater comfort during long hours at the monitor.

Prescription sunglasses

UV protection and vision correction in a single pair of sunglasses, available in different tints and prescriptions.

Our story

A family workshop, with expertise and passion

Ottica Becucci was founded in 1953 and has since offered its customers the most suitable technical solutions to correct any kind of visual defect: from short-sightedness to long-sightedness and high astigmatism.

We craft custom progressive lenses and post-surgery solutions, stock contact lenses of every duration and a wide range of sunglasses and frames. We pay close attention to the latest trends, taste and needs of every customer.

Our consultant Martin guides you in choosing the style that suits you best, always within your budget.
