About us

A family story dedicated to your eyesight

Ottica Becucci was founded in 1953 and has since offered its customers the most suitable technical solutions to correct any kind of visual defect: from short-sightedness to long-sightedness and high astigmatism.

We craft custom progressive lenses and post-surgery solutions, stock contact lenses of every duration and a wide range of sunglasses and frames. We pay close attention to the latest trends, taste and needs of every customer.

Our consultant Martin guides you in choosing the style that suits you best, always within your budget.
